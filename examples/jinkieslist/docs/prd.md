## Summary

Households waste food and make extra trips because nobody knows what is
already at home or what someone else already bought. JinkiesList gives a household
one shared grocery list that updates on every member's phone as they shop,
and a pantry that remembers what is at home and what is about to expire. It
succeeds when a household makes fewer duplicate purchases and throws away
less food. Payments and ordering from stores are out of scope.

## Problem

Household members shop separately. Today they coordinate through group chats
and paper lists, which go stale the moment someone is in a store. In twelve
interviews in August 2026, nine households reported buying something another
member had bought the same week, and seven reported throwing away food they
had forgotten about.

## Users

- **Household organizer:** sets up the household, invites members, and plans
  most of the shopping.
- **Household member:** adds items, shops, and checks items off.

## Goals and metrics

| Goal | Metric | Target | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fewer duplicate purchases | Items checked off twice within 7 days, per household per month | Half the baseline measured in M1 | proposed |
| Less wasted food | Pantry entries removed as expired, per household per month | 30% below the M2 baseline | proposed |
| Households keep using it | Households active in week 8 after joining | 40% | proposed |

## Scope

In scope: a shared list, a pantry with expiry dates, staples that restock the
list, and meal plans that feed the list.

Out of scope: payments and store ordering, because they need retailer
agreements JinkiesList does not have; nutrition tracking, because it serves a
different job.

## Requirements

<a id="r-list-01"></a>
### R-LIST-01 — Members share one list that updates live

- **Priority:** must
- **Status:** accepted
- **Users:** household member

Acceptance criteria:

1. An item one member adds appears on every other member's online device
   within 3 seconds.
2. An item checked off by one member shows as checked for every member.

<a id="r-list-02"></a>
### R-LIST-02 — The list works without a connection

- **Priority:** must
- **Status:** accepted
- **Users:** household member

Acceptance criteria:

1. A member with no connection can add, edit, and check off items.
2. Changes made offline reach other members within 3 seconds of reconnecting.
3. When two members edit one item offline, neither change is silently lost.

<a id="r-list-03"></a>
### R-LIST-03 — An organizer can invite members by link

- **Priority:** must
- **Status:** accepted
- **Users:** household organizer

Acceptance criteria:

1. An invitation link joins the household when opened within 7 days.
2. An organizer can revoke an unused link.

<a id="r-pantry-01"></a>
### R-PANTRY-01 — Checked-off items move to the pantry

- **Priority:** must
- **Status:** accepted
- **Users:** household member

Acceptance criteria:

1. Checking off an item offers to add it to the pantry, with its quantity.

<a id="r-pantry-02"></a>
### R-PANTRY-02 — Members see what expires soon

- **Priority:** must
- **Status:** accepted
- **Users:** household member

Acceptance criteria:

1. Pantry entries expiring within 3 days appear first, marked as expiring.
2. A member can turn on one daily reminder listing entries that expire today.

<a id="r-pantry-03"></a>
### R-PANTRY-03 — Staples restock the list

- **Priority:** should
- **Status:** accepted
- **Users:** household organizer

Acceptance criteria:

1. When a staple's quantity reaches zero, JinkiesList adds it to the list once.
